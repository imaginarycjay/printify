## Defense Coaching: How to Defend Your AI Disclosure

When the panel reviews this attachment, keep these three points in mind:

1. **"Why are the prompts grouped by topic rather than listing hundreds of raw prompts?"**  
   * *Answer:* *"During our systems analysis and agile development sprints, we used AI iteratively as a sounding board. Listing hundreds of fragmented queries like 'yes' or 'next step' would clutter the disclosure. Per our adviser's guidance, we consolidated our interactions into the substantive domain prompts reflecting our actual intellectual direction."*
2. **"Did AI generate your diagrams or source code?"**  
   * *Answer:* *"No. AI was used strictly for concept validation and flow confirmation. All 19 UML Activity Diagrams, ERD, and Class Diagrams were manually constructed in Draw.io, and all software logic was engineered locally in Laravel/Livewire."*
3. **"Are all 20 cited references verified?"**  
   * *Answer:* *"Yes. All 20 references in Chapter 2 were retrieved from legitimate peer-reviewed academic publishers (IEEE, ScienceDirect, DOAJ, Scopus-indexed journals). We personally read and verified their empirical findings before synthesizing them into our project."*
