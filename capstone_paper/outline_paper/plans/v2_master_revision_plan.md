## 2. Why Requirements-Based Functional Testing Avoids UERC Ethics Review

Modeled directly on [`outline_example_1.pdf`](file:///home/imaginarycjay/printify/capstone_paper/outline_paper/scratch/outline_example_1.pdf), **PressQueue** remains 100% exempt from University Ethics Review Committee (UERC) human-subject review through five structural safeguards:

1. **`Testing Personnel and Materials` Replaces `Project Participants` (Chapter 3):** Explicitly states that no human respondent sample is required because functional verification evaluates deterministic software behavior against the **17 locked functional requirements (`FR-01`–`FR-17`)** across four developer-operated role accounts (**Super Admin, Printing Shop Owner, Production Staff, Customer**).
2. **Elimination of Field Interviews and Questionnaires in `Data Collection Procedure`:** Derives **12 Smoke Tests (`ST-01`–`ST-12`)** and **46 Functional Tests (`FT-01`–`FT-46`)** directly from the SRS, using synthetic multi-tenant datasets and sample PDF/PNG/DOCX print files, recording outcomes objectively (`Passed`, `Failed`, `Needs Revision`).
3. **`Data Analysis Procedure` Replaces `Statistical Treatment`:** Removes weighted mean and Likert tables; evaluates conformance by comparing observed outputs against expected acceptance criteria and confirming all failed cases pass **Regression Testing (`Appendix H`)**.
4. **Explicit `Out-of-Scope` Boundaries (Chapters 1 & 2):** Declares that respondent-based usability, user-acceptance, or post-deployment operational-effectiveness surveys are excluded.
5. **ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119 Anchoring:** Grounds verification methodology in **ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119-1:2022**, **29119-2:2021**, and **29119-3:2021**.
