## 2. Why Requirements-Based Functional Testing Avoids UERC Ethics Review

In USM BSIS capstone guidelines (as demonstrated in [outline_example_1.pdf](file:///home/imaginarycjay/printify/capstone_paper/outline_paper/outline_example_1.pdf)), a project triggers **University Ethics Review Committee (UERC)** clearance whenever it collects primary data from human subjects (e.g., pre-development workplace interviews/observations or post-development survey questionnaires with external shop owners, staff, and customers).

To make **PressQueue** 100% exempt from human-subject ethics review while remaining technically rigorous for a Multi-Tenant SaaS platform, v2 applies the exact structural safeguards from `outline_example_1.pdf`:

1. **Replace `Project Participants` with `Testing Personnel and Materials` (Chapter 3)**:
   - Explicitly state that the functional-testing procedure does not require a human respondent sample because it verifies deterministic software behavior against approved functional requirements (`FR-01` to `FR-22`) rather than collecting subjective user ratings.
   - The project developers prepare, execute, and document the test cases across four role-based test accounts (**Super Admin, Printing Shop Owner, Production Staff, and Customer**), while the adviser and panel members review the test plan and execution records.
2. **Eliminate Human Field Interviews and Questionnaires in `Data Collection Procedure` (Chapter 3)**:
   - Remove all references to interviewing or observing 3 local printing shops in Kabacan or distributing ISO/IEC 25010 survey forms.
   - Define data collection strictly as **Requirements-Based Test Execution**: deriving test cases (`ST-01` to `ST-12` and `FT-01` to `FT-45`) from the SRS, preparing synthetic tenant data and sample print files (PDF/PNG/JPEG), executing step-by-step role workflows, and recording observed outputs (`Passed`, `Failed`, `Needs Revision`).
3. **Replace `Statistical Treatment` with `Data Analysis Procedure` (Chapter 3)**:
   - Remove weighted mean ($\bar{x}$) and 5-point Likert scale tables.
   - Evaluate test results by comparing observed system behavior against expected acceptance criteria for every test case and verifying that any failed test case passes subsequent **Regression Testing (`RT-____`)**.
4. **Declare Explicit `Out-of-Scope` Boundaries (Chapter 1 & Chapter 2)**:
   - In `Scope and Limitations — Out-of-Scope` and `Research Gap`, explicitly state: *"Claims not measured by functional testing: The project does not include a separate respondent-based software-quality, usability, user-acceptance, or post-deployment operational-effectiveness study."*
5. **Anchor Quality Assurance in `ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119` (Chapter 2 & Chapter 3)**:
   - Replace the ISO/IEC 25010 survey literature with a dedicated RRL subsection on **Functional Testing and Quality Assurance** citing **ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119-1:2022** (General Concepts), **ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119-2:2021** (Test Processes), and **ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119-3:2021** (Test Documentation).
